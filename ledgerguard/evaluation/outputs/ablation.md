## What each arm removes

- **`rules_only`** — No AI at all. Anything the arithmetic cannot prove is escalated.
- **`no_shadow_ledger`** — No independent expectation; trust the provider's own arithmetic. Only settlement-internal consistency and bank agreement remain. Both hold for a settlement that is internally tidy and simply wrong.
- **`llm_only`** — No deterministic engine; the model decides every case. The model is given the fee and settlement policies in words and must apply them itself. Exposure is scored with the shadow ledger the arm does not have, so the arm is measured generously. Note the cost shape: this arm needs one model call per record, where the hybrid needs one per ambiguous exception. Scored on the first 40 payments.
- **`hybrid_no_gate`** — AI investigates, but its conclusions are taken on trust. Identical to the full system except that nothing is re-derived from records.
- **`hybrid`** — The full system.
